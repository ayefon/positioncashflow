Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2025

No Data Collection

PositionCashFlow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, and there is no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

On-Device Storage

All position data, trade history, and transaction records are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

The export feature creates a JSON file that you control entirely — where it goes and who sees it is up to you.

Network Requests

The app makes network requests to the Polygon.io API solely to retrieve publicly available stock and option price quotes. These requests include:

  • The stock symbol being queried
  • Your Polygon.io API key (which you provide in Settings)

No personal information, device identifiers, usage data, or any other data is included in these requests. Polygon.io's own privacy policy governs their handling of API requests.

Device Permissions

PositionCashFlow does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, health data, Bluetooth, or any other device capability. The only system feature used is file access when you import CSV files or JSON backups, which is initiated by you through the standard iOS file picker.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social media SDKs. The only external service is the Polygon.io market data API, used exclusively for price quotes.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Because the app collects no data, material changes are unlikely.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at github.com/ayefon/PositionCashFlow.
