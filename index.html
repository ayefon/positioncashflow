Your Trades, at a Glance

PositionCashFlow gives you a clear picture of your stock and option positions. Track every leg of a trade — calls, puts, and shares — from open through close or roll, and see exactly where your cashflow stands.

No account or external service is required. All data stays on your device and the app is fully functional on its own. Live market pricing is available as an optional add-on for those who want it — just add a free Polygon.io API key in Settings.

Features

  • 📈
    Multi-Leg Positions Track calls, puts, and stock shares in a single position with full open/close lifecycle.
  • 💰
    Net Cashflow & Position Value See your running P&L from recorded trades. Enable optional live pricing to see estimated liquidation values.
  • 🔁
    Close & Roll Close individual legs or roll options to a new strike and expiration with one tap.
  • 📄
    ThinkorSwim CSV Import Import your trade history directly from ThinkorSwim CSV exports.
  • 🔒
    Private & Local All data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking.
  • 📦
    Backup & Restore Export all positions to JSON and import them on any device.
